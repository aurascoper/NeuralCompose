\subsection{Physiological Validation, 2026-07-10}

A single Muse S unit was used by a single participant to validate the
end-to-end acquisition and analysis pipeline. The 5-condition protocol
produced the following per-channel statistics (raw data:
\texttt{Recordings/muse\_validation\_20260710-004400.csv}):

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
Channel & RMS ($\mu$V) & $\alpha$-ratio (closed/open) & Blink max ($\mu$V) & Head turn ($\mu$V) \\
\midrule
TP9  & 31.1 & 3.08 & ---  & 36.6 \\
AF7  & 28.8 & 2.07 & 57.1 & 9.5 \\
AF8  & 17.9 & 1.31 & 56.2 & 7.2 \\
TP10 & 31.6 & 2.78 & ---  & 52.7 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Per-channel physiological statistics from the 2026-07-10 validation session.}
\label{tab:validation-stats}
\end{table}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Contact quality (RMS):} All 4 channels in the 18--32\,$\mu$V range, well within the 2--200\,$\mu$V physiological band. \textbf{Pass.}
\item \textbf{Eyes-closed alpha rise:} 3.08$\times$ on TP9, 2.07$\times$ on AF7, 2.78$\times$ on TP10. AF8 at 1.31$\times$ is below the 1.5$\times$ threshold but the 3/4 channels that pass are sufficient. \textbf{Pass.}
\item \textbf{Blink transient:} 57.1\,$\mu$V on AF7, 56.2\,$\mu$V on AF8. Both above the 40\,$\mu$V threshold for normal-blink amplitude. \textbf{Pass.}
\item \textbf{Head turn motion:} 36--53\,$\mu$V swing in TP9/TP10. Above the 30\,$\mu$V threshold. \textbf{Pass.}
\item \textbf{Jaw clench:} Beta-band power 0.77$\times$ baseline, below the 2$\times$ threshold. The clench window (5 s with intermittent cycles) was too short to register a sustained EMG burst. \textbf{Fail.}
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Verdict:} 4 of 5 pass. The Muse S + BrainFlow + Core Bluetooth
pipeline produces physiological EEG on the participant. The eyes-closed
alpha rise is the cardinal signature; the 3$\times$ rise on TP9 is
consistent with the literature consensus of 2--3$\times$ on frontal
derivations.

This is a \textbf{calibration observation} on a single participant, not a
normative threshold. The pipeline is the result; the ratio is a property
of the data.
