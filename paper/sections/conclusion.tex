\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The NeuralCompose platform is an open-source, privacy-preserving macOS
artifact for EEG-driven communication and sleep-cycle research. The
hardware-and-software pipeline --- Muse S headband, BrainFlow, Core
Bluetooth, the Swift acquisition stack --- has been validated end-to-end
on consumer hardware. The physiological validation protocol confirms
that the pipeline produces classifiable EEG: a single-participant session
on 2026-07-10 showed an eyes-closed alpha rise of approximately
3$\times$ on TP9, 2$\times$ on AF7, and 3$\times$ on TP10, consistent
with the literature consensus for frontal derivations.

The platform includes a Sleep Validation Toolkit, anchored by the
3D depth-stacked \texttt{EEGScalpPlotterView}, that provides a debugging
surface for multi-session experimentation. The architectural
specification (\texttt{SLEEP\_CYCLE\_DESIGN.md}, $\sim 1{,}500$ lines)
documents the full D1--D8 design with type signatures, hypothesis
registry, risk register, safety requirements, and integration
checklist.

The empirical question --- whether AI-assisted cognitive incubation
during sleep improves creative problem solving --- is presented as a
pre-registered research program (D8, within-subject crossover, $N=30$
target). The D8 study is pre-data-collection. The platform ships
regardless of the outcome; the empirical question is separate.

The repository, the architectural specification, the validation toolkit,
and this draft manuscript are open source at
\url{https://github.com/aurascoper/NeuralCompose}. The platform is
useful independently of the empirical question, and the empirical
question is open.
