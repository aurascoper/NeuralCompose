\begin{abstract}
\noindent
NeuralCompose is a privacy-first, fully on-device macOS research platform that
acquires electroencephalography (EEG) from a consumer-grade Muse S headband,
classifies sleep stage from 4 frontal channels, and integrates audio cues
with a local large language model (LLM) for dream-incubation and dream-report
analysis. The platform is implemented in Swift 6 with strict concurrency, with
all LLM and Core ML inference running locally on the Apple Neural Engine
(ANE). The current contribution is the platform itself: a reproducible
software artifact (open source), a documented architecture
(\texttt{SLEEP\_CYCLE\_DESIGN.md}), and a physiological validation protocol
demonstrating the Muse S + BrainFlow + Core Bluetooth pipeline produces
classifiable EEG on consumer hardware (eyes-closed alpha rise of
approximately 3$\times$ on a single-participant validation session). A
Sleep Validation Toolkit, including a 3D depth-stacked EEG plotter, supports
multi-session debugging. The empirical claim that AI-assisted cognitive
incubation during sleep improves creative problem solving is presented as a
pre-registered research question (D8, within-subject crossover, $N=30$ target)
and is explicitly not a result of the platform. The contribution is the
platform; the empirical question is separate.
\end{abstract}
