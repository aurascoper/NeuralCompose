## Timing: cold load, warm load, throughput, memory

Pull `cold_load_time`, `warm_load_time`, `peak_rss_mb`, and the per-prompt
`tokens_per_second` from `data.json` for every non-skipped candidate. Which
candidates clear NeuralCompose's "next-word latency stays under 200ms"
target (see `MODEL_SETUP.md`) for the carousel path, and which don't? Note
any candidate reported as `"skipped: not downloaded"` — those simply have no
data yet, not a bad result.

## Manual scoring: `scoring-template.csv`

Fill in the six blank columns (`meaning_preserved`, `grammar`,
`no_hallucination`, `no_added_commentary`, `conciseness`,
`instruction_followed`, all 1-5) for every row. Which candidate wins on
average? Does the winner change by prompt category (e.g. a candidate that's
great at `concise-rewrite` but weak on `instruction-following`)?

## Instruction-following regression check

The `instruction-following` category exists specifically to catch a
reintroduced decoding-loop regression (the bug `MLXGenerationRegressionTests`
guards against for Qwen). Did any candidate produce a short-period decoding
loop or otherwise degenerate output on these five prompts? A candidate that
fails here is disqualified regardless of how it scores elsewhere.

## Meaning preservation vs. `word_count_ratio`

For the rewrite-shaped categories (`filler-removal`, `punctuation-restoration`,
`capitalization`, `concise-rewrite`, `command-reformulation`,
`technical-term-preservation`), does `meaning_preservation_cosine` track your
own manual `meaning_preserved` score, or do they diverge in a way worth
noting (e.g. a high cosine score on an output that actually changed the
meaning)? Also check `technical-term-preservation` rows specifically — did
the domain term (EEG/BrainFlow/ANE) actually survive the rewrite unchanged?

going in — including things that turned out *better* than expected, not
